### 3. Findings

**Maturity (issue):**
- **F1 — OA is not populated.** No operational entities, activities or capabilities beyond the root activity. Needs-level context is absent.
- **F2 — LA is thin.** One placeholder component (`LC 1`), no interfaces, and functions that mirror SA one-to-one, so the logical layer adds no decomposition yet.
- **F3 — Requirements coverage is minimal.** One requirement against 27 capabilities and three functional chains. Trace status unknown (not exposed by the tools).
- **F4 — No SA missions** defined; the 27 capabilities are flat with no grouping.

### 4. Overall assessment

The model is **strong at the physical layer and for the system-level function/capability set, and sparse above and between them.** PA has a real hardware decomposition with links and 11 diagrams; SA has a detailed function and capability set. OA is empty, LA is a pass-through, requirements are nearly absent, and naming hygiene needs a cleanup pass. There are no structural errors the tool can detect.

### 5. Follow-ups (proposed, none started)

1. Naming cleanup pass (F5 to F8): fix SA first, then LA and PA, as one change set.
2. Resolve Q1 to Q4 with the model owner; remove confirmed duplicates only after checking `is_actor` and realization links.
3. Decide how far OA and LA should be developed, and populate or deliberately scope out.
4. Expand requirements and record trace links.
5. Re-run this review after changes and compare to this baseline (`d6654d6`).
